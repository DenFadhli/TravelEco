# TravelECO

TravelECO (The Geo Tagging & Carbon Footprint Based Ecotourism Marketplace Platform) is an innovative solution aimed at promoting sustainable tourism practices while creating economic opportunities for local communities. The problem statement is the lack of a comprehensive platform that allows tourists to easily identify and book ecotourism experiences that have minimal impact on the environment and provide benefits to the local communities.
The rationale for this platform is based on the growing concern for the impact of tourism on the environment and local communities. The tourism industry is responsible for significant carbon emissions, habitat destruction, and cultural erosion. Furthermore, the economic benefits of tourism are often not equitably distributed, leading to the exploitation of local resources and people. Build a user-friendly and intuitive platform that connects tourists with local communities in Indonesia, providing them with access to sustainable and eco-friendly tourism experiences. Our platform will leverage geo-tagging and carbon footprint technology to ensure that tourists can make informed decisions about the environmental impact of their travel choices, and allow tourists to contribute to conservation and empowerment efforts in local communities.
By creating a platform that contains package purchases at tourism sites that are not widely known or visited by many people by applying information about the Carbon Footprint at the location, providing rewards in the form of points when buying packages based on categories, and as a form of support for users to create a sense of care for the surrounding environment. A Login and Register Account feature will be implemented to allow users to create and access their own accounts. Secondly, a feature will be implemented to display a list of tourist destinations. Thirdly, a detailed feature for each tourist destination will be provided, which will include information about the location as well as the ability to purchase travel packages. Lastly, the application will also provide information on the carbon footprint at each location.
